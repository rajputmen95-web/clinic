Our Services

Comprehensive Medical Services Under One Roof

From routine health check-ups to specialised consultations, we offer a full spectrum of healthcare services for your family.

General Consultation

Expert diagnosis and treatment for common illnesses, chronic conditions, fever, infections, and general health concerns by experienced physicians.

Advanced Diagnostics

In-house diagnostic services including blood tests, urine analysis, ECG, X-ray, and imaging. Fast, accurate results you can trust.

Pediatrics

Specialised care for infants, children, and adolescents including vaccinations, growth monitoring, and treatment of childhood illnesses.

Pathology Lab

Comprehensive pathology services with accurate, timely reports. Blood counts, lipid profiles, thyroid tests, diabetes panels, and more, all in-house.

Physiotherapy

Evidence-based physiotherapy for musculoskeletal pain, sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, and mobility improvement.

Health Checkup Packages

Customised full-body health packages for individuals, couples, and corporates. Early detection, wellness monitoring, and lifestyle counselling.
